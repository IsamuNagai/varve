title: Why I'm building Varve
date: 2026-10-30

Bitcoin is the most important monetary experiment of our lifetime. It proved that money can be scarce, open and owned by no one. But every coin in it is protected by elliptic-curve signatures, and a large enough quantum computer would break them. Nobody knows when that day comes. It might be decades away; it might never come. What I do know is that money meant to be held for generations should not depend on one cryptographic assumption lasting forever.

So I asked a simple question: if you designed neutral digital money today, for a 50 to 100 year horizon, what would it look like? Varve is my answer.

- **Post-quantum from the first block.** Every coin is protected by ML-DSA or SLH-DSA, the signature standards NIST finalized in 2024. There will never be legacy coins to rescue.
- **Cryptography that can evolve.** Every output records which scheme protects it, so new schemes can be added and weak ones retired by a planned upgrade instead of a crisis.
- **A launch nobody can game.** The genesis block is derived from six future Bitcoin blocks. Not even I can start mining early. No premine, no allocation, no presale. Ever.
- **Small enough to verify.** Capacity is set by what an ordinary laptop can check.

Everything is being built in the open: the specification, every design decision, the simulations and the code. I would rather be proven wrong in public now than after launch. If you see a flaw, open an issue. That is the most valuable thing anyone can do for Varve right now.
