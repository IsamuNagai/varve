FAQ

Common questions, answered straight.

Is there a coin I can buy?

No. Mainnet has not launched, and when it does, coins will exist only through mining. There will never be a presale, ICO, premine, founder or team allocation, or airdrop. Anyone selling "Varve" or "VARV" today is a scammer.

Is there a contract address?

No, and there never will be. Contract addresses belong to tokens that live on another blockchain, such as Ethereum or Solana. Varve is its own blockchain, like Bitcoin. Any "Varve token" or "VARV contract" is a scam.

What stage is the project at?

The specification is written, three independent implementations of the rules agree on every test vector, and the reference node, miner and wallet work. The testnet genesis is fixed, and the public testnet opens with its first seed node. A public, named security audit and the fair launch come after that.

Can I mine?

Soon, on the testnet. Testnet coins have no value: they exist so anyone can test the software, try to break it, and help harden it before mainnet. Mining uses ordinary CPUs. Instructions will appear here and in the journal when the testnet opens.

Will Varve really protect against quantum computers?

Varve uses ML-DSA and SLH-DSA, the post-quantum signature standards NIST finalized in 2024 after years of public analysis. They are designed to resist known quantum attacks, and SLH-DSA relies only on hash functions, a very conservative assumption. No cryptography is proven unbreakable, which is exactly why Varve uses two independent families and can retire a weakened scheme by a planned upgrade. Nobody knows when large quantum computers will arrive; Varve is built so that it does not need to know.

Is it better than Bitcoin?

It makes different choices. Against quantum computers its design is stronger: there are no elliptic-curve coins to migrate. Bitcoin, on the other hand, has seventeen years of battle-testing and enormous mining power, and a young network has to earn that kind of trust. Varve's goal is not to replace Bitcoin but to offer sound money built for the next century from day one.

Can the creator mine early?

No. Mainnet genesis is derived from six future Bitcoin blocks, so nobody, including the creator, can know it or mine on it before those blocks exist.

Does Varve support smart contracts or tokens?

No. There is no smart-contract VM and no token platform. Native timelocked recovery paths are the extent of its scripting, by design: less surface, fewer ways to fail.

What are the units?

The unit is the varve, the smallest unit is the lamina, and 1 varve = 100,000,000 laminae. The ticker is VARV.

Who is behind Varve?

Varve was started by Isamu Nagai, who posts progress in the journal and on X. Everything is open, so the protocol can be checked rather than trusted, and nothing depends on any one person.

How can I help?

Read the whitepaper and the specification, try to break them, and open issues in the repository. Once the testnet opens, run a node and mine. Report vulnerabilities privately as described on the security page.
