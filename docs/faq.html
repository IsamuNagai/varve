FAQ

FAQ

Common questions, answered plainly.

Is there a coin I can buy?

No. No network exists yet and no coin exists yet. There will never be a presale, ICO, premine, founder or team allocation, or airdrop. Anyone selling "Varve" or "VARV" today is a scammer.

What stage is the project at?

Research and specification. Testnet, independent review and mainnet have not started.

Why post-quantum now?

This is not a claim that quantum computers are about to break Bitcoin or any other system. The goal is long-horizon custody: coins held for decades should not depend on signature schemes that may weaken. Varve uses ML-DSA from genesis, with SLH-DSA as an independent recovery family, which gives quantum-resistant signatures under currently understood attack models. Typed outputs and an algorithm lifecycle allow change if those models turn out to be wrong.

Can the author mine early?

No. Mainnet genesis will be derived from a future Bitcoin block header, so nobody, including the author, can mine early.

Does Varve support smart contracts or tokens?

No. There is no smart-contract VM and no token platform. Native timelocked recovery paths are the extent of its scripting ambitions.

What are the units?

The currency unit is the varve, the smallest unit is the lamina, and 1 varve = 100,000,000 laminae. The ticker is VARV.

Who is Isamu Nagai?

Isamu Nagai is a pen name and does not refer to any real person. The protocol matters more than the author, and it is meant to be checked independently rather than trusted.

How can I help?

Read the specification and the whitepaper, review them critically, and open issues in the repository. Report vulnerabilities as described on the security page.
