About Varve

Varve is a specification for a proof-of-work cryptocurrency, developed in the open. The currency unit is the varve (lowercase), the smallest unit is the lamina (1 varve = 100,000,000 laminae), and the ticker is VARV. No coin exists yet.

Design principles

  • Correctness
  • Security
  • Simplicity
  • Cryptographic adaptability
  • Independent verification
  • Predictable issuance
  • Permissionless participation

Design direction

  • UTXO ledger.
  • Signatures: ML-DSA (FIPS 204) from genesis, with SLH-DSA (FIPS 205) as an independent recovery family. These are post-quantum: quantum-resistant signatures under currently understood attack models.
  • Typed outputs and a defined algorithm lifecycle, so algorithms can be introduced and retired in an orderly way.
  • Proof of work: RandomX v2 with a Varve-specific salt, chosen for specification v0.1 and reviewed again before launch.
  • Smooth issuance with a small permanent tail emission.
  • Native timelocked recovery paths.

What Varve deliberately does not build

  • A smart-contract virtual machine.
  • A token platform or a place to issue other assets.
  • A presale, ICO, premine, founder or team allocation, or airdrop.
